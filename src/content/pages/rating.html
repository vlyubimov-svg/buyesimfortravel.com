FAQ

How are the scores calculated?

Six criteria are scored from 0 to 10 and averaged. The criteria and what each one rewards are listed on the methodology page.

Can a provider pay to move up the ranking?

No. Positions are not sold. The disclosure page explains how the site is funded and what that means for links.

Why is a provider with a lower daily allowance ranked first?

Because the total averages six criteria and price per day, payment options and flexibility weigh as much as the daily allowance. A heavy streamer should read the daily data column first.

How current are the prices?

Prices are listed as of October 2026 and change often. Always confirm the final figure at checkout.

Why only five providers?

The rating covers providers with a published unlimited Europe plan and sources we could attribute. More can be added once their plan details can be sourced.
