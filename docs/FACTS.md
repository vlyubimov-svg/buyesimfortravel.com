## General
- A: No Europe eSIM is unlimited at full speed; every plan has a daily allowance or fair use policy.
- A: Rule of thumb: unlimited pays off above about 1.5 to 2 GB per day; below that a fixed plan is cheaper. Check usage: iPhone Settings > Mobile Service; Android Settings > Network > Data usage; divide last month by 30.
- A: 512 kbps / 1 Mbps keep maps, messaging, email, web pages working; video calls and HD streaming do not run smoothly.
- V: EU "Roam Like At Home" applies in the 27 EU states plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway; Ukraine and Moldova covered from 1 January 2026. It does not apply in the UK. Operators may apply fair-use limits on data; Switzerland not mentioned on the page. Source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/roaming (2026-10-27)
- V: Apple: Dual SIM (incl. eSIM) needs iPhone XS, XS Max, XR or later; to use two carriers the carrier must unlock the iPhone, or both plans from same carrier. Source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317 (2026-10-27)
- V: Android hotspot: swipe down, tap Hotspot; Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, USB tethering options. Source: https://support.google.com/android/answer/9059108 (2026-10-27)
- A: Check phone supports eSIM and is not carrier-locked.
